# Phase-I Aptitude — Qualifying-Section Drill Bank

These five sections are **qualifying**, so the goal is safe clearance, not maximum marks.

## Reasoning — 20 marks

High-frequency drills:

- Seating arrangement: linear, circular, two-row
- Puzzles: floor, box, scheduling, attribute matching
- Syllogism: all/some/no/only-a-few cases
- Blood relations and coded relations
- Direction sense and ranking
- Inequality and coded inequality
- Alphanumeric series
- Data sufficiency

## Quantitative Aptitude — 20 marks

High-frequency drills:

- Simplification and approximation
- Number series: missing and wrong term
- Quadratic comparison
- Data interpretation: table, bar, line, pie, caselet
- Percentage, profit-loss, ratio, average
- SI/CI, mixture/alligation
- Time-work, speed-distance, ages
- Mensuration basics

## English Language — 30 marks

- Reading comprehension
- Error spotting
- Single/double fillers
- Cloze test
- Para jumbles
- Sentence improvement
- Vocabulary in context

## Computer Knowledge — 20 marks

- Hardware/software basics
- OS, files, shortcuts
- MS Word/Excel/PowerPoint basics
- Internet, email, networks
- Cyber-security and digital-payments basics
- Database and cloud basics

## Decision Making — 10 marks

- Choose the most practical and ethical action
- Avoid extreme, emotional, or rule-breaking options
- Prefer actions that solve the immediate issue and prevent recurrence
- Institutional process > personal convenience

## Minimum daily drill

```text
10 Quant + 10 Reasoning + 10 English + 5 Computer/DM
Target: finish qualifying sections safely, then return to merit sections.
```
