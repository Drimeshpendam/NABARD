# ESI Core Checklist

ESI carries **40 marks** in Phase I and half of Mains Paper II.

## Macroeconomics

- GDP, GNP, NDP, national income methods
- Nominal vs real GDP
- Growth vs development
- Inflation: CPI, WPI, demand-pull, cost-push
- Deflation, stagflation, disinflation
- Business cycle basics

## Monetary and fiscal policy

- RBI functions
- Repo, reverse repo, CRR, SLR, OMO
- Monetary policy transmission
- Budget: revenue, capital, fiscal deficit, primary deficit
- Direct vs indirect tax
- Fiscal responsibility basics

## Banking and finance

- Types of banks and banking terms
- Priority sector lending
- Financial inclusion
- Non-performing assets
- Basel norms basics
- Digital payments, UPI, DBT
- Financial markets and regulators

## Indian social issues

- Poverty lines and multidimensional poverty
- Inequality and inclusive growth
- Unemployment types and labour-force measures
- Education, health, gender, demographic dividend
- Migration, urbanization, social justice
- Human development and welfare schemes

## Reports and data

- Economic Survey
- Union Budget
- RBI reports
- NABARD annual report
- Census and major surveys
- Indices relevant to poverty, hunger, development, gender, rural India

## Descriptive-ready themes

- Rural credit and farm distress
- Agriculture income and productivity
- Climate risk and sustainable agriculture
- Financial inclusion and last-mile banking
- Employment, skilling, and rural livelihoods
- Digital public infrastructure in rural India
