# ARD Core Checklist

ARD carries **40 marks** in the Phase-I merit section and appears again in Mains Paper II.

## NABARD and rural finance

- NABARD establishment, ownership, functions, subsidiaries
- Refinance, microfinance, rural infrastructure, development role
- Cooperative banks: SCB, DCCB, PACS
- RRBs, commercial banks, priority sector lending
- SHGs, JLGs, MFIs, BC model
- Rural Infrastructure Development Fund and financial inclusion

## Agriculture fundamentals

- Soil types, soil health, land degradation
- Irrigation, watershed, micro-irrigation
- Seeds, fertilizers, pesticides, IPM
- Major crops, cropping patterns, agro-climatic zones
- Horticulture, plantation, forestry, agroforestry
- Animal husbandry, dairy, poultry, fisheries
- Climate-resilient and sustainable agriculture

## Rural development

- Poverty, livelihoods, rural employment
- MGNREGA and rural wage employment
- Rural housing, sanitation, drinking water
- Rural industries, MSMEs, khadi, handicrafts
- Rural infrastructure and connectivity

## Agriculture marketing and post-harvest

- APMC, mandis, eNAM
- FPOs and farmer collectives
- Warehousing, cold chain, storage
- Food processing and value addition
- Agri-export zones and supply-chain basics

## Schemes and institutions

- PMFBY and crop insurance
- Soil Health Card
- PM-KISAN
- Kisan Credit Card
- PMFME
- Agriculture Infrastructure Fund
- Rural employment and livelihood missions
- Ministry-linked schemes and launch years

## High-yield static tables to memorize

- Scheme → ministry → launch year → objective → beneficiary
- Institution → headquarters → role
- Crop → season → major producing states
- Rural body → function → financing/refinancing role
