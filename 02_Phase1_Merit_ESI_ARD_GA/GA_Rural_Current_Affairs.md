# GA + Rural Current Affairs

GA carries **20 marks** in the Phase-I merit section. For NABARD, prioritize rural economy and institutions over generic trivia.

## Daily capture format

```text
Date:
Headline:
Why it matters:
Linked ministry/institution:
Scheme/report/index:
Possible MCQ:
```

## Priority sources of questions

- NABARD announcements, reports, leadership, programs
- RBI policy and rural-finance updates
- Agriculture and rural-development schemes
- Union Budget and Economic Survey highlights
- Reports, indices, rankings related to development
- Appointments and awards relevant to economy/agriculture
- Major crop, climate, food-security, and rural-infrastructure news
- Government schemes: launch year, ministry, beneficiary, feature

## Weekly recall drill

Every Sunday, write from memory:

1. 10 schemes
2. 10 rural/economy facts
3. 5 reports or indices
4. 5 appointments/awards
5. 3 NABARD/RBI developments

Then open notes and mark misses in `06_Mocks_and_Practice/Error_Log.md`.
