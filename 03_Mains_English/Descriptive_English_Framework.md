# Mains Descriptive English Framework

Paper I is **100 marks, 90 minutes, 3 questions**. It tests drafting, clarity, grammar, and relevance.

## Likely task families

- Essay
- Précis
- Comprehension
- Formal letter/report-style drafting

## Essay structure

```text
Introduction       — define the issue + state the angle
Context            — why it matters for India/rural India
Body 1             — benefits/problems with evidence
Body 2             — challenges or counterpoint
Way forward        — practical policy/action steps
Conclusion         — balanced closing line
```

## Strong essay themes for NABARD

- Doubling farmers' income vs sustainable agriculture
- FPOs and collectivization of small farmers
- Role of NABARD in rural transformation
- Climate-resilient agriculture
- Digital public infrastructure and rural finance
- Water-use efficiency and micro-irrigation
- Women-led rural development
- Food processing and rural employment

## Précis method

1. Read once for the central claim.
2. Read again and mark the argument chain.
3. Reduce to about one-third length.
4. Use your own words; no examples unless essential.
5. Give a short neutral title.

## Scoring habits

- Write every week, not only before Mains.
- Time-box each answer.
- Prefer clear paragraphs over ornamental vocabulary.
- Use one relevant scheme/example where it genuinely supports the answer.
