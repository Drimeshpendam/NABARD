# NABARD Awareness — Interview Base

Prepare to explain NABARD's role in simple language, not just memorize facts.

## Core questions

- What is NABARD and why was it created?
- Difference between NABARD, RBI, SEBI, and commercial banks
- What is refinance and why does rural India need it?
- How does NABARD support SHGs, FPOs, cooperatives, and RRBs?
- Major NABARD initiatives in agriculture, microfinance, climate finance, and rural infrastructure
- Current chairman, headquarters, ownership, subsidiaries
- How can a B.Sc. Agriculture graduate contribute to NABARD?

## Your degree angle

Connect your background to:

- Crop and soil knowledge
- Farm-level constraints
- Rural credit and insurance
- Sustainable and climate-resilient agriculture
- Field-level implementation of schemes
- Data, monitoring, and farmer communication

## Interview answer formula

```text
Direct answer → NABARD link → field-level example → balanced closing
```
