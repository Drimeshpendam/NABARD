# ESI + ARD Descriptive Answer Framework

Paper II has **50 objective marks + 50 descriptive marks**. Descriptive is where preparation must become output.

## Answer template

```text
Definition/context — 1–2 lines
Current relevance — scheme/report/data point
Body — 3–5 dimensions or causes
Challenges — real constraints, not generic lines
Way forward — practical institutional/policy steps
Conclusion — balanced and forward-looking
```

## 15-mark answer

- 250–350 words
- Clear subheadings or strong paragraphs
- At least one relevant scheme/institution/example
- A concrete conclusion

## 10-mark answer

- 180–250 words
- Direct answer first
- 3–4 organized points
- Avoid unnecessary background
