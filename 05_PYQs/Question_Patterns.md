# NABARD Grade A — Question Patterns

## Phase I merit section

### General Awareness — 20 marks

High-yield patterns:

- Scheme → ministry → feature → beneficiary
- NABARD/RBI function or recent action
- Reports/indices → rank + publishing body
- Agriculture/rural-development current affairs
- Banking and financial-awareness terms

### ESI — 40 marks

High-yield patterns:

- Concept definition: GDP, inflation, poverty, unemployment
- Policy instrument: repo, CRR, fiscal deficit, subsidy
- Scheme objective and ministry mapping
- Report/index data
- Cause-effect questions on inclusion, employment, development
- Current-linked static economy questions

### ARD — 40 marks

High-yield patterns:

- Crop, soil, irrigation, and agronomy basics
- Rural institutions and refinance structure
- Schemes: PMFBY, PM-KISAN, KCC, PMFME, eNAM, SHG programs
- Animal husbandry, fisheries, horticulture, agroforestry
- NABARD functions, subsidiaries, reports
- Sustainable agriculture and climate resilience

## Mains descriptive patterns

- Policy-analysis questions on rural credit, farm income, climate risk, inclusion
- Scheme-evaluation questions requiring benefits + gaps + way forward
- Current-affairs-linked essays or structured answers
- NABARD-specific institutional questions

## Trap patterns

- Ministry vs implementing agency swap
- Launch year vs announcement year
- Central sector vs centrally sponsored scheme
- NABARD function vs RBI function
- Similar scheme names with different beneficiaries
- Static concept asked through a current-affairs context
