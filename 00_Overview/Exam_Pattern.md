# NABARD Grade A — RDBS General Exam Pattern

Pattern below follows the latest completed **2025–26 cycle**. Recheck the next notification because NABARD can revise stages, marks, or dates.

## Selection stages

1. **Phase I — Preliminary Exam**
2. **Phase II — Mains**
3. **Psychometric Test** — mandatory, qualifying
4. **Interview — 50 marks**

## Phase I — Preliminary Exam

One combined online objective paper:

- **200 questions**
- **200 marks**
- **120 minutes**
- No sectional timing
- Negative marking: **1/4 mark** per wrong answer

| Section | Questions | Marks | Role |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Reasoning | 20 | 20 | Qualifying |
| English Language | 30 | 30 | Qualifying |
| Computer Knowledge | 20 | 20 | Qualifying |
| Quantitative Aptitude | 20 | 20 | Qualifying |
| Decision Making | 10 | 10 | Qualifying |
| General Awareness | 20 | 20 | **Merit** |
| Economic & Social Issues | 40 | 40 | **Merit** |
| Agriculture & Rural Development | 40 | 40 | **Merit** |
| **Total** | **200** | **200** | |

### The rule that matters

Mains shortlisting is based only on:

```text
GA 20 + ESI 40 + ARD 40 = 100 merit marks
```

You must also clear the minimum cutoff in every section. A strong merit score cannot compensate for a failed qualifying section.

## Phase II — Mains: General stream

| Paper | Type | Questions | Marks | Duration |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Paper I — General English | Online descriptive | 3 | 100 | 90 min |
| Paper II — ESI + ARD | Objective | 30 | 50 | 30 min |
| Paper II — ESI + ARD | Descriptive | 6 asked, attempt 4 | 50 | 90 min |
| **Total** | | | **200** | **210 min** |

- Descriptive answers are typed.
- ESI + ARD descriptive can usually be typed in English or Hindi using the prescribed keyboard layout.
- Descriptive normally contains **2 × 15-mark** and **2 × 10-mark** answers.

## Final merit

```text
Final score = Mains 200 + Interview 50 = 250 marks
```

Phase I marks are used only for screening and are not carried into final merit.
