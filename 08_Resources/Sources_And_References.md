# Sources and References

Use official sources first; coaching material should only compress, not replace, the source.

## Primary sources

- NABARD website, annual report, publications, career notices
- RBI policy statements and financial-inclusion material
- Ministry of Agriculture & Farmers Welfare updates
- Ministry of Rural Development updates
- Economic Survey and Union Budget documents
- PIB releases for schemes and reports

## Study material buckets

- ARD static notes and scheme matrix
- ESI static notes and Indian-economy basics
- Current affairs monthly compilation focused on rural economy
- Quant/reasoning question bank for sectional-cutoff safety
- Previous NABARD Grade A analyses and memory-based questions

## Verification rule

Any scheme fact should have:

```text
Scheme name → ministry → launch year → objective → beneficiary → current update
```

If a fact cannot be traced to an official source, mark it as unverified in notes.
